---
type: concept
title: Perspective-Guided Research
description: >
  Assigning distinct perspectives to parallel research branches broadens the
  questions and evidence gathered before synthesis.
sources:
  - title: "Assisting in Writing Wikipedia-like Articles From Scratch with Large Language Models"
    resource: "Shao et al. (2024), full paper, pp. 1–27"
---

Before searching, derive several roles, affiliations, or topical focuses from
related material and assign one to each research branch. Each perspective
directs attention toward different questions that an unconditioned topic query
may miss. Add a fixed generalist branch for basic facts so diversity does not
erase baseline coverage.

This specializes [branch-solve-merge](../reasoning/branch-solve-merge.md) for evidence
discovery: branches do not independently solve the whole task, but gather
complementary material through
[iterative research conversations](iterative-research-conversations.md) that is
later organized into a shared synthesis.
