---
type: concept
title: Research Outline as a Control Artifact
description: >
  An explicit outline between research and drafting exposes coverage for
  evaluation and controls how evidence is retrieved for generation.
sources:
  - title: "Assisting in Writing Wikipedia-like Articles From Scratch with Large Language Models"
    resource: "Shao et al. (2024), full paper, pp. 1–27"
---

In a long-form research workflow, build and evaluate a multi-level outline
before drafting. Start with a parametric outline as a general organizing frame,
then refine it from the gathered research so topic-specific evidence elaborates
the structure instead of arriving as an undifferentiated retrieval dump.

The outline is both an intermediate quality proxy and an execution plan. It
shows whether research achieved adequate coverage before expensive generation,
and each section can drive
[section-scoped evidence retrieval](../knowledge/section-scoped-evidence-retrieval.md).
Removing this stage can degrade both organization and grounded article quality
even when the underlying research conversations remain available.
