type: concept
title: Human-Agent Knowledge Roles
description: >
  Humans should steer evidence and meaning while agents handle repeatable
  filing, integration, linking, and consistency maintenance.
sources:
  - title: "LLM Wiki"
    resource: "LLM Wiki, Why this works and Note"
---

Divide durable knowledge work according to comparative strengths. Humans
select sources, direct inquiry, judge significance, and resolve consequential
interpretations. Agents perform repeatable bookkeeping: distillation, filing,
cross-referencing, propagation of revisions, and consistency checks.

Encode this division in the
[knowledge maintenance operating schema](knowledge-maintenance-operating-schema.md),
including which mutations require human review. The boundary keeps automation
focused on the [maintenance cost curve](knowledge-maintenance-cost-curve.md)
without treating fluent synthesis as authority over source quality or meaning.
