---
type: concept
title: Knowledge Maintenance Cost Curve
description: >
  Automating repetitive cross-reference and consistency work can keep a
  knowledge store useful after manual bookkeeping would cease to pay off.
sources:
  - title: "LLM Wiki"
    resource: "LLM Wiki, Why this works and Note"
---

As a knowledge base grows, the cost of revising summaries, propagating newer
evidence, recording contradictions, and maintaining links expands across many
files. The perceived value of another small update may not grow at the same
rate, causing humans to defer bookkeeping until the store decays.

An agent can lower the marginal cost of these repetitive, multi-file edits and
make [persistent synthesis memory](persistent-synthesis-memory.md) economically
viable. The cost does not become literally zero: review, model calls, erroneous
edits, and maintenance of the
[operating schema](knowledge-maintenance-operating-schema.md) remain. Measure
the pattern by whether automation reduces total upkeep while preserving
evidence fidelity.
